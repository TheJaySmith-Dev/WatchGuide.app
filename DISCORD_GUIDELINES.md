# WatchGuide Discord Community Guidelines

This document outlines the recommended structure, rules, and roles for the official WatchGuide Discord server.

## 📜 Server Rules

1.  **Be Respectful & Kind**
    *   Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, sexism, racism, or homophobia will not be tolerated and will result in an immediate ban.
    *   Keep debates civil. Disagreements about movies are fine; personal attacks are not.

2.  **Strictly No Piracy**
    *   **Do not** share links to illegal streaming sites, torrents, or pirated content.
    *   **Do not** ask for or explain how to bypass region locks or paywalls illegally.
    *   *Why?* WatchGuide is a legitimate discovery tool. associating with piracy puts the project at risk.

3.  **No Spoilers**
    *   Use spoiler tags `||like this||` for any plot details of movies/shows released in the last 3 months.
    *   Keep spoiler-heavy discussions in the dedicated `#spoiler-zone` channel.

4.  **No Spam or Self-Promotion**
    *   Do not spam messages, emojis, or mentions.
    *   Do not promote other Discord servers or unrelated products without Admin permission.

5.  **English Only**
    *   Please keep conversations in English to ensure our moderators can understand and assist everyone.

---

## 🏗️ Server Structure (Channels)

### 📢 Information
*   `#👋-welcome`: Landing page with server overview and verification bot.
*   `#📜-rules`: Read-only channel containing the rules above.
*   `#📢-announcements`: Official news about WatchGuide updates (syncs with GitHub releases).
*   `#🔗-links`: Useful links (Official Website, GitHub, Trakt/Simkl integrations).

### 💬 Community
*   `#💬-general`: General chat for community members.
*   `#🎬-movie-talk`: Discussions specific to movies.
*   `#📺-tv-talk`: Discussions specific to TV shows.
*   `#🔮-recommendations`: Ask for or give suggestions on what to watch next.
*   `#🍿-spoiler-zone`: Free-talk zone for plot discussions (still be mindful!).

### 🛠️ Development & Support
*   `#🐛-bug-reports`: Report issues with the app (use a template: Device, Browser, Description).
*   `#💡-feature-requests`: Suggest new ideas for WatchGuide.
*   `#🆘-help`: Community support for setting up sync, lists, or general usage.
*   `#💻-github-updates`: Feed of commits/PRs from the repository (via Webhook).

## 🛡️ Roles & Permissions

### 👑 **Admin** (Administrator)
*   **Color**: Gold/Yellow
*   **Permissions**: Administrator (All permissions).
*   **Who**: You and core developers.

### 🛡️ **Moderator** (Manage Messages, Kick/Ban)
*   **Color**: Blue
*   **Permissions**: Manage Messages, Kick Members, Ban Members, Mute Members, Mention @everyone.
*   **Who**: Trusted community members who enforce rules.

### 🌟 **Guide** (Community Helper)
*   **Color**: Green
*   **Permissions**: Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files, Add Reactions.
*   **Who**: Active users who frequently help others in `#help`.

### 👥 **Member** (Verified User)
*   **Color**: White/Grey
*   **Permissions**: Send Messages, Embed Links (in specific channels), Add Reactions.
*   **Who**: Default role for users who have passed verification/agreed to rules.

### 🤖 **Bot**
*   **Color**: Purple
*   **Who**: Automated bots (MEE6, Dyno, GitHub Webhook).
